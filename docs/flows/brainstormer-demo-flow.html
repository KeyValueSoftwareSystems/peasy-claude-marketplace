0 · Set the scene0:00 – 1:00 Explain the pain: ideas arrive as one line, PMs spend hours turning them into tickets and chasing design.
1 · Prompt1:00 – 1:30 Paste the prompt (below). Explicit skill name avoids peasy-design triggering instead. Loads skill, classifies as A · Identity & login, reads question bank.
2 · Round 1: scope1:30 – 3:00 Click answers. Narrate why each question matters (itsme, linking, GDPR).
Gate: 4 scope answers		 Asks 4 multiple-choice questions, Belgian defaults marked Recommended.
3 · Round 2: details3:00 – 4:00 Answer, or say "use recommended defaults" to keep pace.
Optional: skip		 Only asks what round 1 left open. Leftovers go to Open questions, never a round 3.
4 · Brief playback4:00 – 4:45 Read it aloud, confirm.
Gate: confirm / correct		 ≤10-line brief: problem, users, v1 scope, out of scope, decisions.
5 · Design?4:45 – 5:00 Choose "Yes, key screens".
Gate: Yes / one screen / No		 Builds design brief: 3 screens, nl-BE, Voorbeelddata, € XX.
6 · Mockup5:00 – 9:00 Fill the wait: show the question bank and ticket format files, explain progressive disclosure. Invokes peasy-design via the Skill tool. Reads Peasy Design System artifact, builds login, itsme consent + pre-fill, and linked-account screens. Mockup (Design artifact link or HTML file)
7 · Ticket9:00 – 10:30 Fills ticket template: epic, 5 stories, Given/When/Then, nl-BE copy, GDPR, open questions, mockup linked. Jira-style HTML ticket, published as an artifact
8 · Walkthrough10:30 – 12:30 Open ticket. Point at Decisions made (audit trail), one story's error criteria, Open questions with owners, the mockup link. 3–5 line hand-over: links + top open questions.
