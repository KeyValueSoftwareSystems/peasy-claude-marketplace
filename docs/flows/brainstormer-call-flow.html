Peasy Brainstormer Call Flow
peasy-brainstormer · call flow

Does it all run inside Claude?

Yes, from idea to shared ticket and mockup. The one step outside is creating the issues in Jira, because no Jira connector is set up for your org. The clickable questions depend on the app: Cowork has them, other surfaces fall back to a text list.

9 of 11 stepsrun fully inside the Claude workspace
1 step partlyclickable questions depend on the app
1 step outsideJira, until a connector is added
